Privacy Policy

Habivit · Last updated June 2026

The short version

Habivit does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your habits stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. That's it.

What we collect

Nothing. Habivit stores your habit data locally on your device using SwiftData. If you are signed into iCloud, Apple syncs that data to your other devices through your personal iCloud account using CloudKit. We have no access to this data at any point.

Third-party services

Habivit contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools. The only external service used is Apple's App Store for processing the optional one-time purchase, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

The optional Habivit Premium upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store any payment information.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. No data leaves your device as part of this feature.

Children's privacy

Habivit does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.
